Inside the drone

Our own 2.24 m VTOL drone for measuring how much water chaparral shrubs lose between dawn and midday, for planning prescribed burns. Click any part on the drone or in the list to see what it does, why we chose it, what it connects to and where to buy it.

Drag to turn, scroll to zoom. Hover spins the lift propellers; Cruise parks them along the booms and runs the pusher. Every part sits at its CAD position and size.

Six systems

Pick a part on the drone or below; it turns purple and lines show what it is wired or bolted to.

Power

Battery, power module and servo regulator: 648 Wh at 21.6 V, about 56 A in a hot hover.

Lift (hover)

Four motors, props and speed controllers that lift the drone off a hillside and land it.

Cruise (pusher)

The pusher that flies the drone on the wing over the plot.

Flight control

Flight controller, GPS, airspeed, radios and Remote ID.

Control surfaces

Four servos for the ailerons and the arch tail.

Airframe

Printed wing, fuselage and tail on carbon tubes. Takeoff mass 8.80 kg.
